Data sources

Player stats (goals, assists, minutes, appearances, cards, rating) come from the API-Football API, scoped to the 2023-24 UEFA Champions League group-stage teams and their squads.

Market values come from Transfermarkt, which has no official API. Values are scraped from each club's current squad page — and here's the important caveat: Transfermarkt's squad pages reflect who is on the club's roster today, not who was there in 2023-24. Players who have since left a club simply show no market value (roughly 16% of the 2023-24 dataset), and values shown for remaining players are current, not dated to that season. There's no reliable free source for true historical squad valuations, so this is a deliberate, disclosed limitation rather than a bug.

How the value score works

The "value score" is a 0–100 heuristic built entirely from the stats above — it is not a market prediction, just a transparent, explainable index for comparison against real market value. It's built from five ingredients, each turned into a percentile (0–100) or curve within the player's position group:

  • Goals per 90 and assists per 90 minutes, percentile-ranked against other players in the same position group.
  • Minutes reliability — caps out once a player has played the equivalent of 10 full matches (900 minutes), so a small sample of great games doesn't overwhelm the score.
  • An age curve — a small bonus for players 21 or under, a small penalty past 32, reflecting typical value trajectories.
  • Appearances, percentile-ranked, rewarding consistent selection.

Attackers and midfielders weight goals/assists heavily (35% + 25% of the score). Defenders and goalkeepers rarely score or assist, and we don't have defensive stats like tackles or clean sheets to weight on instead — so for those positions, the goal/assist weight is redistributed toward reliability, age, and appearances instead.

The final score is mapped to an "implied value" in euros by scaling it against a rough per-position ceiling (€180m for forwards down to €80m for goalkeepers), calibrated by eye against a handful of known real valuations. This number exists purely so the score can be plotted on the same axis as a real market value for comparison — treat it as an index, not an appraisal.

Known limitations

  • Market values are current, not historical (see above).
  • No defensive-actions data (tackles, interceptions, clean sheets), so defender/goalkeeper scores lean more on reliability and age than on-pitch defensive quality.
  • Scope is the 2023-24 Champions League group stage only — the most recent season the free API tier covers.
